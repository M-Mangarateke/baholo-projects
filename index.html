Company profile

Baholo Projects (Pty) Ltd, also known as Baholo, is an industrial services company based in Johannesburg, Gauteng, and a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor. It was founded in 2016 and formally registered in 2019.

Tshegofatso Goitseone Moleko founded Baholo Projects to help businesses source industrial equipment and complete practical work on site. Baholo works with organisations of different sizes across a range of industries.

The team coordinates supply, fabrication and installation from the initial requirements through quotation, delivery and handover.

Useful answers

Before we begin.

Is Baholo the same company as Baholo Projects?

Yes. Baholo is the short brand name used for Baholo Projects (Pty) Ltd, a South African industrial supply and project services company.

What services does Baholo Projects provide?

Baholo Projects offers industrial procurement, welding and fabrication, pipework and installation, scaffolding services and industrial consulting and project support.

Can Baholo supply materials and carry out the installation?

Yes. Baholo can source components and coordinate fabrication and site installation on the same project. The team confirms the materials, work and timing in the quotation.

Does Baholo Projects cover my site?

Baholo Projects is based in Johannesburg, Gauteng, and works at customer sites. Share the site location when you enquire so the team can confirm whether it can supply or work there.

Is Baholo Projects a B-BBEE contributor?

Yes. Baholo Projects is a South African registered company and a Level 1 B-BBEE contributor.

How do I request a quote?

Use the enquiry form, call 067 616 8687 or email info@baholoprojects.co.za. Include the site location, what you need, any specifications and when you need it.

What are your operating hours?

Monday to Friday, 08:00–16:00. Baholo Projects is closed on Saturday and Sunday.
