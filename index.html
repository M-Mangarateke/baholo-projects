Company profile

Founded in 2016 and formally registered in 2019, Baholo Projects is a South African Level 1 B-BBEE company serving small and large organisations with dependable industrial supply, practical project support and skilled workmanship.

Led by founder and Managing Director Tshegofatso Goitseone Molapisi, the business supports industrial supply, welding and fabrication, consulting, scaffolding and pipework-focused requirements. Each engagement begins by understanding the client specification, site conditions and expected result before the final scope is confirmed.

Baholo combines products, workmanship and responsive follow-through through one accountable relationship, helping clients move from an initial requirement to a clearly scoped quotation and practical delivery plan.
