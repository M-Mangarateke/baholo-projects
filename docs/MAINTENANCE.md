# Maintenance checklist

## Weekly for the first four weeks

- Search Console indexing, sitemap, canonical, 404 and enhancement reports.
- GA4 Realtime and event quality; investigate duplicate or missing lead events.
- Apps Script executions, `System Log`, mail status and obvious spam patterns.
- Public homepage, form fallbacks, navigation, privacy/terms links and HTTPS.
- Business Profile verification/edit status without creating duplicates.

## Monthly

- Test one authorised, labelled enquiry end to end.
- Review Sheet access, lead statuses, retention candidates and mail failures.
- Review search queries and landing pages; improve visible factual content
  rather than adding speculative keywords or schema.
- Check authentic photo/service/hours accuracy on the Business Profile.
- Confirm domain renewal, Pages custom-domain state and certificate health.
- Run broken-link, mobile, accessibility and performance regression checks.

## Quarterly

- Review GitHub, GoDaddy and Google account membership and least privilege.
- Scan current source and history for secrets.
- Review Apps Script scopes, quotas and deployment ownership.
- Check event taxonomy and remove unused/noisy measurement.
- Review third-party policy or consent changes affecting GA4, Apps Script,
  WhatsApp or Business Profile.

## Annually and after material change

- Qualified South African privacy/legal review of the privacy notice, terms,
  POPIA process, retention period and data-subject request handling.
- Verify company identity, contact details, services, B-BBEE statement and
  Business Profile facts against authoritative company records.
- Recovery drill: clone to a clean directory, run locally, validate DNS/HTTPS,
  redeploy Pages and confirm the private lead service can be restored from the
  documented source without exposing credentials.
