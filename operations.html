Baholo Operations

Baholo Operations is the staff portal operated by Baholo Projects (Pty) Ltd. It helps authorised staff manage website enquiries and review aggregate website performance.

Enquiry management Website performance Staff access

Enquiry management

Staff can search the private enquiry register, assign an enquiry, update its contact details, status and notes, and set a follow-up date. The portal records changes for accountability and emails staff when enquiries are assigned to them.

Website performance

The dashboard reads aggregate reports from the Baholo Projects GA4 property. Staff can review website activity and compare sessions and completed enquiries from Google and Bing organic search.

Staff access

Baholo Projects authorises staff individually. Staff use the sign-in link provided by Baholo Projects, sign in to Google and request a one-time email link.

Read the Baholo Projects privacy notice for how the portal uses Google data, and see the website terms. For help, email info@baholoprojects.co.za.
